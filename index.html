About the study

Why are we conducting this study?

Speech emotion recognition systems may perform differently when the same person changes speaking language. This study examines emotional expression in native-language and English speech under controlled recording conditions.

During the study, the same fixed sentence is used across several target emotions within each activity condition. This helps keep lexical content constant while the vocal expression changes.

Research focus

We are interested in whether the relationship between a participant’s self-reported emotion and speech-emotion-model prediction changes when the participant speaks in their native language versus English.

What will you do?

Study procedure

1

Study explanation and consent

Read the study explanation and complete the required informed-consent procedure.

2

Participant setup

The researcher assigns a participant ID and sets the trial conditions.

3

Read and record

Read the displayed sentence while naturally expressing the target emotion. Each recording is approximately 5–10 seconds.

4

Check your recording

Play the audio once. Re-record only if it is too quiet, cut off, or difficult to hear.

5

Self-report

Select the emotion you actually felt and rate how strongly you expressed the target emotion before processing.

6

Repeat the trials

Complete 16 trials covering native-language / English conditions and neutral / happy / sad / angry target emotions.

Privacy and voluntary participation

Your participation is voluntary

Participation is voluntary. Choosing not to participate, or choosing to withdraw according to the approved study procedure, will not disadvantage you in your classes, academic evaluation, supervision, or other university activities.

The research interface uses a participant ID. Do not state your name, student number, address, or telephone number in the recording.

The study application processes recordings locally during the session. Detailed handling, storage, retention, withdrawal, and use of research data follow the approved study documents provided during the consent procedure.

Frequently asked questions

FAQ

Do I need to be good at acting?

No. You will be asked to express each target emotion naturally. This is not an acting-performance evaluation.

Will I see the model’s emotion prediction during the experiment?

No. Model outputs are hidden during the 16-trial collection so they do not influence later recordings or self-reports.

Can I record again?

Yes, but only for technical audio-quality problems such as low volume, a cut-off recording, or audio that is difficult to hear. Do not re-record simply to change the emotional expression.

Do I need special equipment?

No. The researcher will provide the recording setup used for this study.
